## 1. User group

**Who are you designing for?**  
For tourists, visitors and parents with young children.

**Why might this group need support during Diyandi?**  
These users want to have a conducive possible first-time experience while attending Diyandi Festival.

---

## 2. Situation or need

**What is this group trying to do during Diyandi?**  
Users want to maximize their day by being informed of crucial public advisories, knowing which roads are blocked/congested, must-have and prohibited items to bring, time and place of events they'll attend, and food stalls they'll visit.

---

## 3. Problem or inconvenience

**What may make this task difficult, confusing, unsafe, slow, or inconvenient?**  
A large amount of information are made available by local authorities in social media prior upcoming festivals to ensure security and safety, but for some, especially those who do not prioritize scrolling on these platforms on a free time,  getting the vital details in a single scroll may be a hit or miss. A visitor or a parent who is securing the needs of his children may forget to bring other essentials, get their things confiscated, forget to visit pop-ups they wished they had, miss their events, and face unexpected inconvenience in road routes as a result of not getting complete information. 

---

## 4. Proposed digital solution

**What digital tool would you propose?**  
A mobile application named Diyandi Ready Kit that encompasses security guidelines, a heat and medical emergencies reminder, detour roads, and scheduled events and food/refreshments pop-ups in a color-coded, simplified map.

**How would it help the intended users?**  
This digital tool centralizes security prohibitions, event schedules, and other important public advisories into a concise format that eliminates the need for festival-goers to monitor different social media posts/pages and limits missed advisories to prevent having confiscated belongings or missing events they planned to see. It provides heat index advisories, essential belongings checklist and customizable reminder for hydration or medication intake. This also uses a color-coded map in order for users to distinguish designated pop-up locations, medical stations and event venues, and be informed on road blockage and traffic advisories.

---

## 5. Things users can do

Describe **two specific actions** that users could perform using your proposed system.

1. View reminders of security prohibitions ( such as caps, jackets, backpack, sharp object, etc.) and must-bring essentials like water, medication, handheld fan, or a custom list.
2. Locate scheduled events, food stalls and get information of traffic advisories, route layouts and road closures

### Quality 1: Easy to use

This kit should be easy to use and not cluttered because users will need a convenient and easy to read information when moving across places.

### Quality 2: Accessible, low-data

**Why does this matter to users?**  
This kit should be accessible on limited mobile data considering that not everyone prioritizes having fast and stable internet access in such occasions.
